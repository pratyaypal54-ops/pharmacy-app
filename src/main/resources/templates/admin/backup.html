☁ Automated Google Drive Cloud Protection

To guarantee that you never lose data even if your shop PC physically burns or breaks, follow these 2 simple steps to automatically save a copy to your Google Drive:

Step 1: Install Google Drive on PC
Download and install official Google Drive for Desktop. It creates a virtual Google Drive folder on your Windows computer (e.g. G:\My Drive\PharmacyBackups).
Step 2: Save Backups to That Folder
Whenever you download the backup or run the automated script, it drops the .sql file directly into your Google Drive folder. Google automatically uploads it to cloud servers instantly.
Step 3: Instant 2-Minute Restore
If the shop PC ever breaks, you simply log in to your Google Drive on any other laptop, download the latest .sql backup file, and import it into MySQL. Every sale and medicine is 100% restored without paying a single rupee!
